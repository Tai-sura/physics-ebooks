Transverse wave

Particles vibrate perpendicular to the direction of wave travel.

P: moving up P moves only up and down; its x stays fixed. λ is the distance between two neighbouring crests.

Longitudinal wave

Particles vibrate parallel to the direction of wave travel.

P: moving right P moves only left and right; its equilibrium position is fixed. λ is the distance between two neighbouring compressions.
